Stone veneer siding cost in the Lehigh Valley

Real pricing by coverage area, and why installation quality matters as much as the stone itself.

Stone veneer siding in the Lehigh Valley typically runs $15 to $30 per square foot installed, including materials and labor. On a full 2,000-square-foot exterior, that puts total cost around $30,000 to $60,000, though most homeowners use stone veneer on a smaller accent area rather than a full exterior, which brings the total well down.

What drives the price

Coverage area

An entryway, fireplace surround, or foundation accent runs a fraction of what a full exterior costs, simply because there's less square footage involved.

Manufactured vs. natural stone

Manufactured stone veneer is lighter, easier to install, and less expensive — typically at the lower end of the range. Natural stone veneer costs more but offers a look manufactured products can't fully replicate.

Substrate and prep work

Proper installation requires a code-compliant water-resistant barrier and correct flashing before the stone goes on. Skipping this step to save money is the single most common cause of a veneer installation failing early — poor installation can fail within just a few years, with repair costs exceeding what correct installation would have cost in the first place.

Why the Lehigh Valley's climate matters for installation

Stone veneer set with a mortar mix that isn't suited to repeated freeze-thaw cycling is a known failure point locally. A properly installed system, with the right barrier, flashing, and mortar mix, can last 30 to 50 years. A poorly installed one can show cracking and water damage within a handful of winters — which is why the installer's experience with this specific climate matters more than the brand of stone on the price list.

Getting an accurate quote

A trustworthy stone veneer quote should specify coverage area, material (manufactured vs. natural), substrate prep, and flashing details as a fixed price in writing — not just a per-square-foot number with no scope attached.

Key takeaways

  • Stone veneer runs $15–$30 per square foot installed in the Lehigh Valley.
  • Manufactured stone costs less than natural stone but both need correct substrate prep to last.
  • Poor installation is the top cause of early failure — not the material itself.
  • A properly installed system can last 30–50 years in this climate.

FAQ

Is manufactured stone veneer as durable as natural stone?

When installed correctly, yes — both can last decades. Installation quality matters more than the material choice for either.

Should I choose stone or brick veneer?

Depends on budget and coverage area — see our full stone vs. brick veneer comparison.

How long does stone veneer installation take?

An accent area typically takes a few days to a week; full-exterior projects take longer depending on square footage.
