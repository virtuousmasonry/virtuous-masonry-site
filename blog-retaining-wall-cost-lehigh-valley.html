Retaining wall cost in the Lehigh Valley: what it actually runs

Real pricing by size and material, what pushes a quote higher, and why drainage matters more than the block you pick.

Most Lehigh Valley retaining walls run $35 to $50 per square foot installed, all-in with materials, labor, and site prep. A modest wall — 20 feet long and 3 feet tall, or 60 square feet — typically lands around $2,100 to $3,000. Taller walls cost more per square foot, not just more overall, since height adds engineering and reinforcement requirements that a short wall doesn't need.

What actually drives the price

Height

Walls over about 3–4 feet need reinforcement (geogrid, engineered backfill) that shorter walls don't, which can add 30–50% to the per-square-foot cost compared to a shorter wall in the same material.

Site access and soil

A yard with a steep slope, rocky soil, or tight equipment access adds excavation time before any wall material goes in. Excavation on difficult sites can run well beyond the baseline cost of straightforward, flat, accessible yards.

Drainage

Basic drainage is usually built into the per-square-foot price, but the clay-heavy soil common through parts of the Lehigh Valley can require enhanced drainage — this is not an area to cut, since it's the single biggest factor in whether a wall lasts.

Material

Segmental concrete block is the most common and cost-effective choice locally. Natural stone and brick cost more but offer a different look. See our retaining wall materials guide for the full breakdown.

Why drainage matters more than the block you choose

A retaining wall's job is to hold back soil and water pressure behind it. Without a gravel backfill and drainage pipe to relieve that pressure, water builds up behind the wall, freezes in winter, and pushes the wall forward or cracks it — regardless of whether it's built from concrete block, stone, or brick. This is the part of a retaining wall quote that's easy to skip to look cheaper, and the most common reason a wall fails within a few years instead of lasting decades.

Getting an accurate quote

A trustworthy retaining wall quote follows an on-site visit, not a phone estimate, and spells out wall height, material, drainage plan, and total length as a fixed price in writing.

Key takeaways

  • Most Lehigh Valley retaining walls run $35–$50 per square foot installed.
  • Walls over 3–4 feet need reinforcement that adds 30–50% to the per-square-foot cost.
  • Drainage matters more than material choice for long-term durability — don't let a quote skip it.
  • Get a fixed price in writing after an on-site visit that specifies drainage, not just height and length.

FAQ

Do I need a permit for a retaining wall?

Often, yes, once a wall exceeds a certain height — local codes in Allentown, Bethlehem, and Easton vary, and we can tell you what applies to your project on a free on-site visit.

How long does a retaining wall last?

A properly built wall with adequate drainage commonly lasts 30+ years; poor drainage is the most common reason a wall fails much sooner.

What's the difference between a retaining wall and a landscaping wall?
