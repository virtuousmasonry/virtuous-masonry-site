Foundation repair cost in Pennsylvania: what to expect

Typical pricing by severity, what drives the cost up, and how to know what kind of contractor you actually need.

Foundation repair in Pennsylvania typically runs $2,200 to $16,500 depending on the severity of the problem, with most homeowners landing somewhere around $5,000 for a moderate repair. Urban areas with clay-heavy soil, like parts of Philadelphia, tend to run higher ($8,000–$12,500); rural and suburban areas, including much of the Lehigh Valley, more often fall in the $5,000–$9,000 range for a typical repair.

What drives the cost

Type and severity of the problem

A minor crack repair costs far less than underpinning a settling foundation. The specific repair method — sealing a crack versus installing piers to stabilize a section of foundation — is the single biggest factor in total cost.

Excavation required

Reaching a damaged footing or a section that needs underpinning often requires excavation, and how much excavation is needed (and how deep) adds directly to labor cost.

Pier depth

When piers are used to underpin a settling foundation, how deep they need to go to reach stable soil affects both material and labor cost — this varies by property and local soil conditions.

Masonry repair vs. structural foundation work

Not every foundation issue needs a specialty foundation company. Masonry-related problems — a cracked or spalling concrete foundation wall, deteriorating mortar joints in a stone or block foundation, or minor settling cracks — are within a masonry contractor's scope. More severe structural issues, like a foundation that needs piering or major underpinning, typically call for a specialty structural engineer or foundation repair company. Part of an honest first estimate is telling you which category your project actually falls into, rather than quoting a job that's outside the contractor's expertise.

Getting an accurate estimate

A real foundation repair estimate follows an on-site inspection, ideally identifying the underlying cause (drainage, soil movement, settling) and not just patching the visible symptom. A fixed price in writing that specifies the repair method, not just a lump sum, is what to look for.

Key takeaways

  • Pennsylvania foundation repair typically runs $2,200–$16,500, averaging around $5,000 for a moderate repair.
  • Repair method (crack sealing vs. underpinning) is the biggest driver of total cost.
  • Masonry-related foundation issues are within a mason's scope; major structural underpinning usually isn't.
  • Get a fixed price in writing that identifies the actual repair method, not just a lump sum.

FAQ

Is foundation repair covered by homeowners insurance?

Usually not for gradual settling or wear — similar to chimney repointing, insurance typically covers sudden specific damage, not ordinary deterioration.

How do I know if my issue is masonry or structural?

See our guide to signs of foundation problems — a real on-site look is the most reliable way to know for sure.

Does foundation repair cost more in winter?

It can, depending on the repair method and ground conditions — ask about timing when getting your estimate.
